Dress details

For bridesmaids who want something polished, comfortable, and easy to wear all day, this chiffon dress is a safe win. The V neck and wrap-style bodice create a flattering shape without feeling tight, while the airy flutter sleeves give soft coverage and look beautiful in motion. At the waist, the embellished applique detail adds just enough sparkle for wedding photos and helps define the silhouette without needing extra accessories. This dress is made for real wedding pacing—walking down the aisle, mingling at cocktail hour, and dancing without worrying about stepping on your dress.
