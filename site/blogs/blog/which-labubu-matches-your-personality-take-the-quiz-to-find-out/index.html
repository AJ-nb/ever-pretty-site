You’ve seen it. That wide-eyed, slightly grumpy, undeniably adorable creature taking over your TikTok and Pinterest feeds. Labubu isn't just a toy—it's a whole mood. And with so many different styles and personalities, it’s no wonder people are asking:

Which Labubu are you?

Whether you’re bubbly and bright or more of a mysterious soul who thrives after dark, there’s a Labubu that mirrors your vibe. Ready to meet your match?

The Sweetheart: Cotton Candy Labubu

Personality match: Warm, kind-hearted, the friend everyone turns to.

You love soft aesthetics, spontaneous hugs, and handwritten notes. You're the type who remembers birthdays, brings snacks to study group, and sends “just thinking of you” texts.

If Labubu were your spirit animal, you’d be wrapped in fluffy pastels, probably smelling faintly of vanilla, and your superpower would be emotional support with sparkles.

You’d love: Floral dresses, cozy cardigans, soft lighting, romantic comedies on loop.

The Dreamer: Starry Night Labubu

Personality match: Deep thinker, stargazer, late-night soul searcher.

You thrive when the world is asleep. You write poetry no one sees, and you’ve probably cried listening to movie scores. Your mind is full of “what ifs” and beautiful daydreams.

Labubu, in this version, reflects your dreamy solitude and quiet magic.

You’d love: Maxi tulle dresses, galaxy prints, handwritten journals, playlists titled “for when I can’t sleep”.

The Rebel: Punk Labubu

Personality match: Bold, expressive, a little chaotic—in the best way.

You don’t follow trends—you are the trend. With a sharp wit and a soft heart (well hidden), you challenge norms and make your own rules. You speak up, stand out, and sometimes set things on fire (figuratively, we hope).

Punk Labubu’s messy hair and mischievous smile are your energy in toy form.

You’d love: Combat boots with tulle skirts, band tees, nose rings (real or fake), saying “I don’t care” but actually caring deeply.

The Nature Child: Forest Labubu

Personality match: Grounded, calm, connected to the earth.

You’re the friend who suggests a hike on a Sunday morning, who owns more plants than furniture, and finds peace in long walks with no destination. Your vibe is all about inner calm and outer curiosity.

This Labubu belongs in mossy woods, maybe sipping tea from an acorn cup.

You’d love: Earth-toned outfits, natural fabrics, campfire playlists, incense and rain-scented candles.

The Quiet Creative: Mono Labubu

Personality match: Observant, introverted, but bursting with ideas.

You're the type who fades into the background at parties but ends up being the most interesting person in the room. You communicate best through art, design, or a perfectly timed meme. Quiet? Yes. Boring? Never.

Mono Labubu’s minimalist color and subtle expression are made for those who don’t shout to be heard.

You’d love: Black-and-white aesthetics, clean silhouettes, museum dates, curated Pinterest boards with no title.

So...Which Labubu Are You?

Maybe you're the Punk on the outside, but secretly the Starry Dreamer. Or the Sweetheart who craves solitude like Forest Labubu. No matter your vibe, there’s a version of Labubu that gets you—flaws, quirks, soft spots and all.

And honestly? That’s what makes Labubu more than a trend. It’s a tiny mirror of us: playful, moody, emotional, unexpected.
