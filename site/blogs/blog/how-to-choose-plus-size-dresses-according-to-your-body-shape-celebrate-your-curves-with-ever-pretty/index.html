Formal occasions require all of us to dress smart outfits that make us look great. It is often common that plus size women have a hard time trying to achieve this as sometimes brands do not adapt to their needs, making the task of finding the perfect plus size dress an ordeal.

Whether you’re looking for a plus size bridesmaid dress, a plus size wedding dress or any other plus size formal dresses, Ever Pretty will accompany you through the whole process to make sure you totally nail it!

Key Takeaways (Quick Answers)

If you want a more elongated look, start with a V-neckline and a defined waist.

For tummy-friendly comfort, choose chiffon or stretch fabrics that drape instead of cling.

If you prefer arm coverage, try long sleeves, flutter sleeves, or slit sleeves (coverage without bulk).

For curves you want to highlight, lean into wrap bodices, A-line skirts, and mermaid silhouettes (when the fabric has give).

When in doubt, pick a color that photographs well: black, green, blue, burgundy, or pink.

How to Identify Your Body Type

If you want to do your best to flatter your figure, you might want to identify your body type before selecting the right dress. These tips will help you!

Measure your body. Having your measurements will not only help you identify your body shape but also choose the correct size when purchasing it.

If your shoulders and hips have a similar width together with your waist, then your body type is rectangle. However, if your belly is a bit more pronounced, your shape is a circle. The hourglass shape is characterized by similar width in shoulders and hips but with a small waist. Pear shaped bodies have a small torso and prominent hips. Inverted triangle body shaped bodies, on the other hand, tend to have wide shoulders and torso but small, narrow hips. Take a look at the picture for guidance.

The best styles for this body shape are V-necklines that make your torso look longer, short dresses that show your legs and A-line dresses.

A-line and off-shoulder dresses help slim your shape in a natural way and highlighting your waist will add the final sexy touch, cutting the triangle shape in a nice way.

What you need to do if you have a rectangle shape is simply exaggerate your natural curves and create a deep waistline. You can also add texture and volume to give a natural feminine shape.

Factor 3: Color

Wondering the trendy colors are this year? We’ve got 5 winners.

1. Black: The forever-classic. Black is sleek, polished, and instantly event-ready—and it’s one of the easiest colors to style up or down. If you want a subtle plus size wedding guest look that still feels confident (and photographs like a dream), black is the safe bet that never feels boring.

2. Green: Green is the “quiet luxury” color family—elegant, fresh, and flattering on a lot of body shapes. Emerald green brings that rich, formal glow, sage green and eucalyptus feel softer and romantic, and olive green gives a more earthy, modern vibe. If you want color without stealing the spotlight, green does the job beautifully.

3. Blue (this year’s trend): If there’s one color family showing up everywhere this year, it’s blue. Navy blue is the classic alternative to black, dusty blue feels calm and wedding-friendly, and lighter shades like sky blue are perfect for spring and summer ceremonies—especially outdoors. Trendy, wearable, and easy to match with silver or neutral accessories.

4. Burgundy: Burgundy is subtle but powerful. It looks expensive in photos, feels made for fall and winter weddings, and pairs beautifully with gold jewelry. If you want a romantic color that doesn’t feel overly sweet, burgundy is a strong choice.

5. Pink: Pink can go soft, modern, or bold—depending on the shade. Think blush pink, dusty rose, and light pink for a gentle, romantic feel, or hot pink and fuchsia when you want more of a statement. It’s a great pick for “pink wedding guest dress” shoppers who still want something flattering, not flashy.

FAQ

1) What dress silhouette is most flattering for plus size women?
A-line and wrap styles are the easiest winners because they define the waist and skim the hips without clinging. If you want more curve emphasis, a mermaid silhouette can also work—just choose a fabric with stretch or a forgiving overlay.

2) What neckline makes you look slimmer in a plus size dress?
V-necklines are a go-to because they elongate the neck and draw the eye upward. Off-shoulder necklines can also be very flattering when you want to highlight collarbones and balance wider hips.

3) What’s the best plus size dress style to hide belly or feel tummy-friendly?
Look for a higher or defined waist (wrap, empire, or belted styles) plus drapey fabrics like chiffon. Avoid stiff fabrics that sit right on the midsection or ultra-thin materials that cling.

4) Which fabrics are most comfortable for plus size formal dresses?
Chiffon and stretch fabrics tend to feel the most forgiving for all-night wear. If you want a more polished finish, satin and jacquard can look elevated—just prioritize good structure and a comfortable lining.

5) Can plus size women wear mermaid dresses?
Absolutely. The trick is fit and fabric: choose a mermaid style with stretch, supportive construction, and a comfortable lining so it hugs curves without feeling restrictive.

6) How do I choose a plus size wedding guest dress if I want arm coverage?
Go for long sleeves, flutter sleeves, or slit sleeves—they give coverage while still looking light. A V-neck or open neckline helps keep the look balanced so it doesn’t feel “heavy” on top.

7) What length is best for plus size formal occasions?
For formal and black tie optional events, floor-length gowns are the safest choice. Midi lengths work beautifully for cocktail or semi-formal weddings—especially when the hem hits at a flattering spot (not the widest part of the calf).

8) What colors photograph best for plus size wedding guest dresses?
Black is always classic, but deep tones like navy blue, emerald green, and burgundy look especially rich in photos. If you want something softer, dusty blue, sage green, blush pink, or dusty rose tend to look romantic without washing out.
