FAQS

Are these dresses suitable for different body types?

Our choir dresses are designed to accommodate various body shapes and sizes. We offer a range of sizes and often provide customization options to ensure the perfect fit.

How do I select the right size for my choir members?

We have a detailed size chart available for each dress style. Simply follow the measurements guide to determine the best fit for your choir members.

Can I request customization or alterations for the dresses?

Yes, we offer customization services based on specific requests. Please reach out to our customer service team to discuss any alterations or custom designs.

What fabrics are used for the dresses?

Our choir dresses come in various fabrics like chiffon, sequin, lace, etc. Each dress description specifies the fabric used, ensuring you know the material before purchase.

Do you offer bulk or group discounts for choir orders?

Yes, we provide discounts for bulk orders. Reach out to our sales team for information on group discounts and special offers.
