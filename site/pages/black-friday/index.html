When is black friday 2025?

Black Friday in 2025 falls on November 28th. On that day, we will have even greater discounts than ever before. Of course, Ever-Pretty's Black Friday extends beyond just that day. We will be planning various promotions starting from the weeks leading up to Black Friday. For specific event details, you can subscribe to our newsletter to receive firsthand information.

When is cyber monday 2025?

Cyber Monday in 2025 falls on December 1st. On that day, you can expect to find fantastic discounts and special offers on our website. But at Ever-Pretty, Cyber Monday is more than just a single-day event. To get all the insider details about our Cyber Monday deals and offers, we encourage you to subscribe to our newsletter. Subscribers will be the first to receive exclusive information about our upcoming promotions and savings, ensuring you don't miss out on any of the great deals we have in store.

Can I use coupon codes or other discounts during the Black Friday sale?

Absolutely! During our Black Friday sale, we offer various discounts in the form of coupons, price reductions, and more.
