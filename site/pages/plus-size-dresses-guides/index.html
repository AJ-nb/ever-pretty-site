Tips for Choosing The Right Plus SizeFormal Dresses For Your Body

Silhouette styles play a crucial role in highlighting or de-emphasizing various features, making the choice of plus size formal dresses particularly important. When selecting the perfect dress, it's essential to consider your body shape. Here, we've broken down five of the most common body types and recommended dress styles to help you discover the ideal fit for plus size formal dresses

Rectangle

Characters: undefined waist, same size bust and hips, lack of curves

Recommended A-line V-neck

Apple

Characters: broad shoulders, carrying most of weight in tummy and top half of the body, undefined waist

Recommended Empire waist V-neck Asymmetrical

Hourglass

Characters: Shoulders and hips are fairly balanced, with a defined waist

Recommended Mermaid Sheath

Pear

Characters: Narrow shoulders, wide hips, thick thighs and legs and small defined waist

Recommended A-line Strapless off the shoulder

Strawberry

Characters: Shoulders are broader than hips,large bust and long slender legs

Recommended V-neck A-line Sweetheart Neckline
