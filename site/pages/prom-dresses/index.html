FAQ

When is prom?

Most proms take place between late March and July, around the end of the exams or summer term. The exact date is determined by the school.

What is the best time to shop for a prom dress?

Proms are typically held from March to July. If you want to shop online, it’s best to do it in October to December. If you’d rather shop in-store, try January to February.

How much are prom dresses?

A prom dress can cost anywhere from $100 to $800, depending on the type, fabric and brand of the dress. For example, some designer gowns range from $500 to $600, whereas some brands offer affordable gowns under $100.

How to dress for a prom?

Elegant formal gowns or modest evening dresses are the most obvious choices. However, short prom dresses with a sexy or fashion-forward edge tend to do well as well. Check with your school or the venue to make sure the prom dress you choose meets the dress code and represents your personal style.

What’s the difference between prom and homecoming?

Three main differences between prom and homecoming:

1.The date. Proms are generally held from March to June, while homecoming usually occurs in September or October, which is often viewed as a kind of welcome-back activity.

2.Participant. Prom is for seniors and sometimes juniors,while homecoming is open to all students.

3.Dress code. Prom is typically very formal, while homecoming is usually much more casual.

Should prom dresses be long or short?

As proms tend to be formal affairs, girls generally dress in long gowns. However, short dresses have grown in popularity at proms lately. Short prom dresses are totally acceptable as long as they meet dress code standards.

Could I wear a sexy prom dress?

What prom dress styles are most flattering?

The most flattering prom dress styles are those that emphasize the body’s best features. So it’s best to start by learning your body type and know what you want to showcase. An A-line dress will draw attention to the upper body while hiding the butt, while a mermaid prom dress will accentuate them.
